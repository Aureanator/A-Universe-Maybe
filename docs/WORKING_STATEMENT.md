## The statement

0. **Axiom.** Directed implications reduce. — AXIOM

1. **One quantity.** There is one fundamental quantity: implication. It is
   conserved exactly and no information is destroyed. — POSTULATED

2. **Reduction is activation; reductions are ticks.** "Reduced" means an edge
   becomes active and carries state. Each reduction is a complete logical event,
   and the sequence of reductions is the clock. Nothing is ever still: energy is
   implication in motion at the one speed. — POSTULATED

3. **Light cones (reading 1).** Each event's influence spreads equally in all
   directions at that speed. Drawn in spacetime, the resulting cone has a 90°
   opening angle: the light cone. For a source moving at that speed there is no
   trailing cone; the wake and the wavefront coincide. — POSTULATED.
   In three space dimensions, massless waves from a brief flash travel exactly
   on the cone and leave no trailing wash (the sharp Huygens principle; it
   fails in two dimensions). Waves of anything with mass also fill the inside
   of the cone: mass produces a wake. — DERIVED (standard; checked on the
   project's mesh by P18)

4. **Every route, with phase.** An implication proceeds along every available
   route. Each wavefront carries a phase and an internal orientation
   (polarisation/spin), and contributions combine as signed amplitudes. The
   group labels on edges supply the holonomy along each route. — POSTULATED.
   Signed waves (real or complex) are *required* for trapping: probability
   weights provably always escape any region with an exit (P17, T1), while real
   and complex waves both trap their exit-free modes (P17 T3, P18 T3′). — DERIVED

5. **Vacuum is complete cancellation.** Where every loop closes (full
   reduction), all routes agree and the wakes cancel. Residue that does not
   cancel is charge. — existing definitions, DERIVED within the model

6. **Matter is trapped circulation.** A particle is a pocket where
   implication circulates without escaping. Rest mass is the energy of that
   circulation. — POSTULATED.
   Exact form: modes that vanish at every exit never leak, and symmetry and
   degeneracy guarantee them. A slight asymmetry turns permanent trapping into
   slow decay. — DERIVED (P17, T3–T6)
   **Mass (user's definition, 2026-09-19).** Inertial mass is the rewrite cost
   of *translating* the structure. Equivalently, it is the maintenance cost in
   implication steps relative to a free implication crossing the same
   neighbourhood. Both equal the energy of the trapped circulation. The
   light-clock argument gives the equivalence, and M1 = M2 in
   `DYNAMICS_DESIGN.md` §3 is the test. — POSTULATED definition; equivalence
   DERIVED (sketch)

7. **Hierarchy.**
   - free light
   - bonds and mechanical tension: metastable exchange loops between pieces of matter
   - matter: the same thing, smaller and far more stable

   — POSTULATED; OPEN

8. **Decay is escape.** Nothing is destroyed: a particle decays when its
   trapped implication finds a way out.
   - A finite trap gives a finite lifetime. With probability weights it grows
     exponentially in trap depth or code length; with amplitudes on a fractal
     it grows doubly-exponentially. — MEASURED (P17)
   - Only exact cancellation, or unlimited depth, is permanent. — DERIVED / MEASURED (P17)

9. **Reversible globally, irreversible locally.** The whole world evolves
   reversibly, but undoing a local event needs the entire world state. A
   region's own evolution is therefore that of an open system.
   - The leak model used in P17 is exactly this local description.
   - Matter is a pocket of local reversibility: modes that do not couple to the world.

   — POSTULATED; DERIVED consequences (P17)

10. **No local scratchpad.** History exists only as the present configuration.
    A resettable memory would destroy information (Landauer), unless the
    forgotten bits are radiated away at a cost in implication. So trapping must
    come from the structure of the present state, not from stored history. —
    DERIVED (reasoning), consistent with P17

11. **Observation** extracts finite-precision information from the record.
    Outcomes are intervals, never completed points; the continuum is refinement
    that never finishes. Probability = |amplitude|² is still assumed. —
    POSTULATED; the Born weight is OPEN (CLAIMS row 16)

12. **References exist only between structures.** There is no background
    grid, stage or rest frame. The mesh is itself made of implications, and its
    regular starting form is only a fixture.
    - **Distance** is causal delay between structures.
    - **Orientation** is comparison by carrying a frame along the edge labels
      (A4 = rotations of a tetrahedron). The result is path-dependent where
      curvature intervenes; absolute orientation is meaningless.
    - **Motion** is change in relations over a structure's own ticks.
    - **Isotropy** means no structure can detect a preferred direction by
      comparing delays.
    - Drawing coordinates are a human chart only. **Every test reports
      relational quantities.**

    — FOUNDATIONAL (README architecture rule 4; memo §2); made explicit here

## Amendment (2026-09-20, after P27–P29): clause 6 narrowed

Clause 6 said matter is trapped circulation. Three results narrow it; the earlier wording is kept
above as written.

1. **Strictly trapped states cannot circulate** (Astra, DYNAMICS_DESIGN §15; Opus, §16). A state
   supported only on a loop, with exits available at its vertices, has exactly zero net probability
   current — including every 0/π superposition of such states, which are standing waves rather than
   circulations.
2. **What a compact loop does have** is an internal return: on an odd loop whose holonomy is an
   order-2 element, the internal vector comes back with a minus sign, so two traversals return it.
   Invisible to probability; the first structure here of the kind the electron target needs.
3. **Circulation requires a tail and spin-½** (§17). Stationary flow lives only in degenerate
   eigenspaces; with K² = +1 disorder removes the degeneracy and with it all circulation. The 2T
   spinor lift gives K² = −1 and Kramers degeneracy, and circulation returns — carried by the field
   around the loop (≤ 8.5 % of the weight on the loop itself).

**Clause 6, as it now stands:** matter is a compact core whose *field* circulates; the core's own
signature is an internal return, not a probability current; and this requires the spin-½ lift of the
labels. Self-binding (why the core holds at all) remains open, as does the axiom-level reason for
the lift.


## Amendment 2 (2026-09-20): the axiom restated, and clause 6 replaced

**Axiom, in the user's words:** causality travels to local effect, and it is always travelling as a
conserved quantity. A multidimensional causal structure can enumerate irreducible, self-referential
links.

This makes local conservation a **constraint at each vertex** (A_v), not a cost. With flatness
(B_f) it gives H = −Σ A_v − Σ B_f, which is gapped. See `docs/CORRECTIONS_2026-09-20.md`.

**Clause 6 now reads:** matter is a **defect in the constraints** — a charge is a vertex where local
conservation fails; a flux is a face where flatness fails, and in three dimensions the flux of a
single edge is the closed ring of faces around it. Defects are created in pairs at the ends of
strings, are stable because they cannot be removed locally, and move. The earlier readings
("trapped circulation", then "a compact core whose field circulates") are kept above as history and
are superseded: a compact stationary loop carries no current, so under this axiom it is not matter.
