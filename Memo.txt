# 1. Core Thesis

Build physics from one axiom:

> **Directed implications reduce.**

Meaning: if an implication is binding and its antecedent is resolved, the consequent is resolved. Space, time, charge, matter, quantum behavior, and gravity are not assumed; they are attempted to be derived from discrete event/constraint relations.

The framework is called **Event-Constraint Dynamics**.

# 2. Basement: Minimal Event-Causal Structure

We begin with only:

\[
\mathcal{E} = \text{set of events}
\]

and a directed implication relation:

\[
\to \subseteq \mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{E}.
\]

A realized reduction is a special implication:

\[
R \subseteq \to,
\]

written:

\[
A \Rightarrow B.
\]

A resolution state is:

\[
S \subseteq \mathcal{E}.
\]

The closure map is:

\[
F_R(S)=S \cup \{B \mid \exists A \in S,\ A \Rightarrow B\}.
\]

The deterministic local axiom is:

\[
A \in S,\ A \Rightarrow B \implies B \in S.
\]

From realized reductions, define causal precedence:

\[
A \preceq_R B
\]

iff there is a finite chain:

\[
A=e_0 \Rightarrow e_1 \Rightarrow \cdots \Rightarrow e_n=B.
\]

This is a preorder. Cycles are allowed. Define causal equivalence:

\[
A \sim_R B \iff A \preceq_R B \text{ and } B \preceq_R A.
\]

Then emergent time is the quotient poset:

\[
\mathcal{T}_R = \mathcal{E}/\sim_R.
\]

**Achievement:** time is not a background parameter; it is the partial order generated by realized reductions.

# 3. Constraint Labels, Holonomy, and Charge

Introduce a group \(G\). Assign to each directed implication \(X \to Y\) a constraint label:

\[
A_{XY} \in G,
\]

with reverse direction:

\[
A_{YX}=A_{XY}^{-1}.
\]

For a directed cycle:

\[
C=(v_1 \to v_2 \to \cdots \to v_n \to v_1),
\]

define holonomy:

\[
\Phi_C = A_{v_1v_2}A_{v_2v_3}\cdots A_{v_nv_1}.
\]

A gauge transformation is a map:

\[
\lambda:\mathcal{E}\to G,
\]

acting as:

\[
A_{XY} \mapsto \lambda_X^{-1}A_{XY}\lambda_Y.
\]

Then:

\[
\Phi_C \mapsto \lambda_{v_1}^{-1}\Phi_C\lambda_{v_1}.
\]

So the conjugacy class \([\Phi_C]\) is gauge-invariant.

Define:

- **Full reduction:** \(\Phi_C=e_G\).
- **Partial reduction:** \(\Phi_C \neq e_G\).

The residue is:

\[
Q_C = \Phi_C.
\]

Physical charge is the gauge-invariant conjugacy class:

\[
[Q_C].
\]

**Achievement:** charge is not substance; it is nontrivial holonomy, i.e. a topological obstruction to full cyclic reduction.

Charge conservation follows if a region’s boundary constraint is fixed. Internal reconfiguration cannot change boundary holonomy:

\[
\Delta \Phi_{\partial R}=e_G.
\]

This is the discrete analogue of boundary-of-boundary cancellation.

# 4. Dimensional Selection: Why 3D?

The minimal relational structure supporting nontrivial cyclic closure is the tetrahedral boundary:

\[
\partial \Delta^3.
\]

It has:

- 4 vertices,
- 6 edges,
- 4 triangular faces.

Its orientation-preserving automorphism group is:

\[
A_4.
\]

The natural representation of \(A_4\) is obtained from:

\[
\mathbb{R}^4
\]

by quotienting the diagonal:

\[
W=\mathbb{R}^4/\mathbb{R}(1,1,1,1).
\]

Thus:

\[
\dim W=3.
\]

The crucial identity is:

\[
\wedge^2 W \cong W.
\]

This is special to three dimensions because:

\[
\dim(\wedge^2 W)=\frac{d(d-1)}{2},
\]

and requiring:

\[
d=\frac{d(d-1)}{2}
\]

gives:

\[
d=3.
\]

This isomorphism gives the cross product and the Lie algebra:

\[
\mathfrak{so}(3).
\]

Translations are then associated with the next simplex boundary:

\[
\partial \Delta^4,
\]

whose symmetry structure leads, in a local flat contraction, to:

\[
\mathfrak{e}(3)=\mathbb{R}^3 \rtimes \mathfrak{so}(3).
\]

So the proposed sequence is:

\[
C_3 \to \partial\Delta^3 \to \partial\Delta^4 \to \mathfrak{e}(3).
\]

That is:

\[
\text{cycle} \to \text{rotation} \to \text{translation} \to \text{3D Euclidean geometry}.
\]

A second argument is topological stability: knots are stable in 3D but trivial/unstable in 2D and 4D.

**Achievement:** 3D is selected as the unique dimension where relational closure, rotational structure, and stable topology coexist.

F:\mathcal{S}\to\mathcal{S}

F(O)\cong O.

\text{Matter} = \text{persistent relational fixed point of reduction}.

\[
\tau(F(O))=\tau(O).

\tau(O)\neq \tau(\emptyset),

Mass is defined as structural resistance to reconfiguration:

**Achievement:** matter is not substance in space; it is a recurrent topological event-pattern. Mass is the cost of deforming that pattern.

# 7. Internal Resolution and Matter vs. Light

A single tetrahedron is too small for genuine internal dynamics because all edges are boundary data.

Two tetrahedra sharing a face still do not necessarily provide independent internal edge variables under the original edge-holonomy model.

The minimal improved object is a cone over a tetrahedral boundary:

\[
v * \partial\Delta^3.
\]

This adds an interior vertex \(v\) joined to the four boundary vertices, creating genuine internal degrees of freedom.

After gauge fixing, there are again:

\[
12^3=1728
\]

raw internal resolutions for a fixed boundary.

The key distinction becomes:

- **Light/direct implication:** one unique internal resolution for a given boundary.
- **Matter:** multiple inequivalent internal resolutions for the same boundary.

Formally:

\[
\mathcal{I}(B)=\frac{\text{admissible internal resolutions of }B}{\text{boundary-indistinguishable equivalence}}.
\]

If:

\[
|\mathcal{I}(B)|=1,
\]

the process is effectively direct/light-like.

If:

\[
|\mathcal{I}(B)|>1,
\]

the process carries hidden internal state, i.e. matter-like structure.

**Achievement:** matter is identified with irreducible internal ambiguity under boundary constraint.

\[
H=C_G(q).

\[
H \cong \mathbb{Z}_3.

The set of internal triples \((H_1,H_2,H_3)\) satisfying:

\[
H_1H_2H_3=q

has size:

\[
|A_4|^2=144.

The permutation representation of \(\mathbb{Z}_3\) on these 144 states decomposes as:

\[
54V_0 \oplus 45V_1 \oplus 45V_2,

where \(V_0,V_1,V_2\) are the three one-dimensional irreps of \(\mathbb{Z}_3\), with phases:

\[
1,\omega,\omega^2.

For \(q\) of order 2, the centralizer is:

\[
V_4 \cong \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2,

and the analogous decomposition is:

\[
48W_0 \oplus 32W_1 \oplus 32W_2 \oplus 32W_3.

**Achievement:** quantum-like sectors arise as irreducible representations of the residual constraint group.

Interactions are represented by tensor products:

\[
\mathcal{H}_1 \otimes \mathcal{H}_2.

Allowed outcomes correspond to invariant/singlet couplings.

Measurement is modeled as coupling the system to a dual probe. If the system is in state \(|\psi\rangle\) and \(P_k\) projects onto outcome \(k\), the number of valid resolutions is taken as:

\[
N_k = \operatorname{Tr}(P_k|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)=|\langle k|\psi\rangle|^2.

Thus:

\[
P(k)=|c_k|^2.
\]

**Achievement:** the Born rule is proposed as a counting rule over valid bilinear constraint resolutions, not as a primitive postulate.

\[
\rho_0.

\text{curvature of coarse-grained event mesh}

**Achievement:** gravity is reframed not as force in spacetime, but as observer-level geometry of variable event/interaction density.
